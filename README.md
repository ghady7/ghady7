## About Me

I’m a data analyst focused on transforming raw data into clear, actionable insights that support better business decisions.

My work spans data preparation, exploratory analysis, KPI reporting, dashboarding, and communicating findings in a way that stakeholders can use immediately.

### Core strengths
- SQL and data querying
- Python for analysis and automation
- Power BI and dashboard storytelling
- KPI tracking and business reporting
- ETL/data pipeline workflows
- Translating business questions into data-driven solutions

### Current focus
- Customer and operational analytics
- Product and marketing performance analysis
- KPI dashboards and executive reporting
- Trend analysis and forecasting
- Data quality and analytics process improvement

## Data Philosophy

I believe strong analytics is built on three pillars:

- Understand the business question clearly
- Validate the data and the assumptions
- Communicate the answer in a way that drives action

This approach helps turn raw numbers into insights that are both credible and useful.
