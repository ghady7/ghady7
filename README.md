## 👋 About Me

I’m a data analyst focused on turning raw data into clear business insights, clean dashboards, and smarter decisions.

I enjoy working across the full analytics lifecycle: collecting data, cleaning it, modeling it, visualizing it, and presenting actionable results to stakeholders.

### What I work with
- SQL for data extraction and transformation
- Python for analysis, automation, and modeling
- Power BI / dashboards for business reporting
- Excel for quick analysis and business summaries
- ETL workflows and data pipeline logic
- Business problem solving with measurable impact

### Current focus
- Data analysis and reporting
- KPI and performance dashboards
- Customer and operational analytics
- Predictive analytics and trend discovery
- Data storytelling for business decisions

I believe great analytics starts with understanding the business question, then building a reliable process to answer it with evidence.

That means:
- asking the right questions
- validating assumptions
- exploring data deeply
- turning findings into clear recommendations
- making insights easy for teams to act on
